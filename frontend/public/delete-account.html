Ledger Pro

Delete your Ledger Pro account

For the Ledger Pro app by BeingReal Studio (Mohammad Ahmad), available on Google Play and at accounts.beingreal.in

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@beingreal.in from the email address registered on your account.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Ledger Pro account. You do not need to give a reason.
  3. We will reply to confirm the request came from you, then delete the account. Deletion is completed within 30 days of confirmation.

If you no longer have access to the registered email address, say so in your message and we will verify your identity another way.

What is deleted

  • Your user account: name, email address, phone number and password.
  • Your company profile, including any logo you uploaded.
  • All bookkeeping data in your company's database: accounts, contacts, transactions, balances, cheque reminders, business items and generated documents.
  • Sign-in tokens for every device.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Server backups that already include your data are retained for up to 30 days after deletion, then overwritten.
  • Emails you sent us about the request are kept as a record of the request itself.
  • If a company account has several team members, deleting your user account removes you from it; the company's books belong to the company and are deleted only when its owner requests it.

Deleting some data without closing your account

You can delete individual transactions, contacts and accounts inside the app at any time. To have specific records removed by us instead, email the same address and describe what should go.
