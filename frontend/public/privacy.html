Ledger Pro

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · Applies to the Ledger Pro mobile app and the web app at accounts.beingreal.in

Ledger Pro is bookkeeping software. It exists to record the financial transactions you enter and to show you reports built from them. This policy explains what information the service handles, why, and what control you have over it.

Who operates Ledger Pro

Ledger Pro is operated by BeingReal Studio (Mohammad Ahmad). Questions and requests about your data: support@beingreal.in.

Information we collect

InformationWhy we need itHow it is collected
Name and email addressTo create and secure your account, verify your email, and contact you about your account.You enter it when you register.
Phone number (optional)Shown on your profile; used only if you choose to add it.You enter it in Settings.
Company detailsCompany name, country, currency and logo appear on your reports and documents.You enter them during setup.

Information we do not collect

  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos or files from your device, other than a company logo you choose to upload.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking. The app does not profile you or sell data.
  • Biometrics: the optional fingerprint or face lock is handled entirely by your device's operating system. The app only receives a yes or no answer and never sees or stores biometric data.

How information is stored and protected

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Each customer's bookkeeping is kept in its own separate database.
  • On your phone, your sign-in token is kept in the device's secure storage, and a copy of recently viewed data is cached so the app works offline. Signing out removes both.
  • Access to servers is restricted to the people who operate the service.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. The only parties that process data on our behalf are the hosting provider that runs our servers and the email provider that delivers verification and reminder emails. Reports or documents you export or share from the app go only where you send them.

Retention and deletion

Your records are kept for as long as your account exists so that your books stay complete. You can delete individual records inside the app at any time. To delete your account and all of its data, email support@beingreal.in from your registered address; we will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep records longer.

Your rights

You can view and correct your profile and company details in Settings, export your reports at any time, and request a copy or the deletion of your data by contacting us. If you are in a region with specific data-protection rights, those rights apply to you regardless of what this policy says.

Children

Ledger Pro is a business tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
